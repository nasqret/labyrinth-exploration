All notable changes to this skill. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org):
the data files (`knowledge.json`, `events.jsonl`, `frontier.json`, `sota.json`) are the
interface.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-08

First public release.

- **Method**: the loop (read the frontier, explore, predict boldly, test and log, referee,
  update the map, look for shortcuts), the hard rules, the session protocol and the
  saturation ladder.
- **Tiers and review states**: T1–T6 keep proof apart from speculation; T2 and T3 results
  also carry a review state (`unreviewed`, `under-review`, `refereed`, `human-checked`).
- **Campaigns**: attack agents, literature agent, referees, writers and a coordinator;
  fill-in briefs in `templates/briefs/`.
- **Engine** (`templates/lab.py`, standard library):
  - a domain-neutral frontier map (`frontier.json`) with a sweep-line classifier and bins
    for large ranges;
  - detection of values claimed both realized and impossible;
  - the state-of-the-art table with history and escaped LaTeX and Markdown output;
  - validation of nodes, events, review states, frontier rows and table rows.
- **Dashboard** (`templates/dashboard.html`):
  - map, barcodes, state of the art, frontier, optional scatter, knowledge graph, timeline,
    board and tables;
  - light and dark mode;
  - every panel fails on its own, with a message;
  - `?tab=` opens a tab directly.
- **Example**: triangle counts in graphs on n vertices, exact for n ≤ 7, with an exhaustive
  test for n = 8.
- **Docs**: README, step-by-step tutorial, example prompts, graphics generated by
  `tools/make_graphics.py`.

The method was developed on a research programme in pure mathematics in September and
October 2026; `references/lessons.md` records its lessons without its results.
